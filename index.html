02 — Services

What we
do

  • 01

    Brand Identity

    Naming direction, logotypes, type systems, colour, and the rules that keep a brand recognisable everywhere it appears.

    A complete identity is less a logo than a set of decisions. We define the mark, the type and colour system, and the rules for how they behave — then document it so the work stays consistent long after handover.

    Typical deliverables

    • Logotype and mark suite
    • Type, colour, and layout system
    • Brand guidelines document
    Discuss this service
  • 02

    Digital Design

    Websites, interfaces, and design systems built around clarity — layout, hierarchy, and interaction that feel considered.

    Digital work starts with structure: what someone needs to understand first, second, and never. We design responsive layouts, component rules, and interaction states that hold up from a 320px screen to a wide desktop.

    Typical deliverables

    • Responsive page layouts
    • Component and state library
    • Prototype and handoff notes
    Discuss this service
  • 03

    Campaigns

    Concept-led campaign work spanning key visuals, art direction, and adaptable formats for launch moments.

    A campaign needs one idea that survives translation across formats. We develop the central concept, the key visual, and the system of crops, cuts, and adaptations that let it travel without thinning out.

    Typical deliverables

    • Campaign concept and key visual
    • Format and channel adaptations
    • Art direction for production
    Discuss this service
  • 04

    Art Direction

    Photography, styling, and visual language direction that gives a project a consistent point of view.

    Art direction is how a project keeps the same point of view across every image. We set the references, the light, the framing, and the styling rules, then review the work against that direction.

    Typical deliverables

    • Visual direction deck
    • Shot list and styling guidance
    • Image selection and retouch notes
    Discuss this service

04 — Studio

A small
practice

Lumen Studio is a fictional independent creative practice exploring how ideas become visible — through type, image, grid, and restraint.

Fictional demo studio. Built as a portfolio exercise.

EST. NOWHERE / FICTIONAL

The studio is imagined as a small, deliberately unhurried team working across identity, digital, campaigns, and art direction. Work begins with questions rather than decoration, and ends with systems a team can actually use.

Every project on this page is invented for demonstration. No clients, awards, statistics, or testimonials are claimed — only the approach itself.

  • A

    Clarity

    If it needs explaining twice, it needs designing again.

  • B

    Curiosity

    Better questions arrive before better answers.

  • C

    Craft

    Spacing, weight, and timing are the work, not the finish.

  • D

    Collaboration

    The strongest direction is the one we can build together.

05 — Process

How the
work moves

  1. 01

    Discover

    Questions, context, and constraints. We learn the terrain before proposing a direction.

    Kickoff conversation, existing-material review, and a written summary of what we heard — so assumptions are visible early.

  2. 02

    Define

    Positioning, principles, and a brief worth designing against — agreed before visuals begin.

    One page of direction: audience, tone, constraints, and the measures we will use to judge the work together.

  3. 03

    Design

    Type, grid, colour, motion, and image developed together into a coherent system.

    Two or three routes explored on real content, reviewed in sequence rather than side by side, then narrowed to one.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final assets, guidelines, and handover — documented so the work keeps working.

    Organised files, usage rules, and a walkthrough session so the system can be extended without us in the room.
